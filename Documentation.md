# FoodBridge AI
### Project Documentation

**Project Name:** FoodBridge AI  
**Project Type:** AI-Powered Food Rescue Platform  
**Application:** Web Application  
**Target:** College Hackathon MVP

---

## 1. Project Overview

FoodBridge AI is an AI-powered web platform designed to reduce food waste by connecting food donors with nearby NGOs and organisations that can distribute surplus food to people in need.

Restaurants, hotels, hostels, bakeries, college canteens and event organisers often have surplus edible food that goes unused.

At the same time, many NGOs and charitable organisations need food resources to support communities.

FoodBridge AI provides a centralised platform where donors can publish surplus food donations, NGOs can discover suitable donations, and volunteers can help coordinate food collection and delivery.

The platform uses an intelligent matching algorithm to recommend suitable NGOs based on food compatibility, quantity, distance, available capacity and collection deadlines.

## 2. Problem Background

Food waste and food insecurity are two important social challenges.

### Problems faced by food donors

- Restaurants and events frequently generate surplus food.
- Donors may not know which nearby NGOs need food.
- Finding suitable recipients manually takes time.
- Delayed communication can result in food being wasted.

### Problems faced by NGOs

- NGOs may not know where surplus food is available.
- Finding suitable food donations can be difficult.
- They need accurate information about food quantity and type.
- They need sufficient time and resources to arrange collection.

### Existing Gap

There is a need for a simple and organised platform that connects food donors and NGOs while helping them coordinate suitable food donations efficiently.

## 3. Proposed Solution

FoodBridge AI aims to solve this problem through an intelligent food donation and rescue platform.

The platform will allow:

1. Food donors to register and publish surplus food details.
2. NGOs to register and manage their food requirements.
3. The system to evaluate donation eligibility.
4. The matching engine to recommend suitable NGOs.
5. NGOs to accept or reject donation requests.
6. Volunteers to coordinate food collection and delivery.
7. Users to track donation progress.

The platform will also provide an impact dashboard to display completed donations and estimated meals rescued.

## 4. Project Objectives

The main objectives are:

- Reduce avoidable food waste.
- Connect surplus-food donors with suitable NGOs.
- Simplify the food donation process.
- Improve coordination between donors and recipients.
- Use intelligent matching to identify suitable recipients.
- Prioritise donations based on collection deadlines.
- Encourage community participation through volunteering.
- Promote responsible food redistribution.

## 6. Main Features

### 6.1 User Authentication
- User registration and login.
- Role-based access for donors, NGOs and volunteers.
- Secure password handling.

### 6.2 Food Donation Management
- Create food donation listings.
- Add food name, category and quantity.
- Specify vegetarian or non-vegetarian type.
- Provide preparation time and collection deadline.
- Manage donation availability.

### 6.3 NGO Management
- NGO registration.
- Organisation information.
- Food preferences.
- Receiving capacity.
- Donation request management.

### 6.4 AI-Based Smart Matching

The matching engine will recommend suitable NGOs by evaluating:

- Food compatibility.
- Available receiving capacity.
- Distance from the donor.
- Collection time feasibility.

The system will generate matching scores and explain why a particular NGO is recommended.

### 6.5 Food Urgency Prioritisation
- Evaluate collection deadlines.
- Identify time-sensitive donations.
- Prioritise donations requiring faster collection.
- Highlight urgent donation requests.

### 6.6 Location-Based Matching
- Display donor and NGO locations.
- Calculate distances.
- Identify nearby eligible organisations.
- Support location-based recommendations.

### 6.7 Donation Tracking
- Track donation status.
- Record acceptance.
- Manage pickup assignments.
- Confirm delivery completion.

### 6.8 Impact Dashboard
Display information such as:
- Total completed donations.
- Estimated meals rescued.
- Active donations.
- Completed pickups.
- Participating organisations.

## 9. Limitations and Assumptions

### Limitations

- The initial application will be a web-based MVP.
- Real-time logistics and delivery tracking will be limited.
- Matching accuracy depends on the quality of entered information.
- The initial system will not independently verify food safety.
- Location accuracy depends on available location information.
- Real-world NGO partnerships are not assumed.

### Assumptions

- Donors provide accurate food information.
- NGOs maintain updated capacity and food preferences.
- Users have internet access to use the platform.
- Volunteers are available when pickup coordination is required.
- Sample accounts and donation records may be used for the hackathon demonstration.

**Food Safety Note:** The platform will collect food preparation, storage and collection information and apply eligibility checks. AI recommendations will not be treated as proof that food is safe to consume. Food safety requirements must be followed throughout collection, storage and distribution.

## 10. Project Success Criteria

The initial project will be considered successful when:

- Donors can register and publish food donations.
- NGOs can register and view available donations.
- The matching engine can identify and rank suitable NGOs.
- The system can explain its matching recommendations.
- NGOs can accept donations.
- Donation statuses can be updated.
- Completed donations appear in impact statistics.
- The complete workflow can be demonstrated successfully.
